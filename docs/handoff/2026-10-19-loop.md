# `<Loop>` (2026-10-19)

`<Loop durationInFrames from? times?>` (`packages/react/src/components.ts`)
repeats its children: every `durationInFrames` frames from `from`, their
clock starts again at 0 (issue #142).

- A loop is two `<Sequence>`s. The outer one carries the loop's props and
  bounds it: `from`, and `durationInFrames = times * period` when `times` is
  finite (otherwise the enclosing window's end). Inside it, `LoopCycle`
  renders the current cycle as `<Sequence from={cycle * period}
  durationInFrames={period}>`. The walker, audio collection, `<Video>`
  timing, subtitles, `openEnd`, and `<FreezeFrame>` therefore need no new
  code: every frame is an ordinary sequence chain.
- `useVideoConfig().durationInFrames` inside is the period, also in a last
  cycle that a fractional `times` or the enclosing window cuts short; the
  outer window clips the layers and audio.
- Audio: each cycle reports a clip starting at the cycle, cut to the loop's
  range. Frames of one cycle report the same clip, which
  `merge_react_audio_clips` collapses; different cycles are different clips.
- `durationInFrames` must be positive and finite (fractions allowed);
  `times` must be positive (`Infinity` is the same as none).
- `isBefore` (`time.ts`): `<Sequence>`, the walker's
  `childSequenceContext`, `<Subtitles>` cues, and `LoopCycle` compare window
  edges with a 10 µs tolerance. Each `<Sequence>` rounds its shifted time to
  a microsecond, so before this, `<Sequence from={0}><Sequence from={10}>`
  skipped frame 10 at 30 fps (0.333333 s < 10 / 30), and a loop's cycles
  lost their first frames.
- The walker ends a `<Sequence>` without `durationInFrames` at its parent's
  window end (`windowEndSec`), as `<Sequence>` does, instead of at the
  parent's range end, which is clipped to the composition. They differed
  only past the composition's end without `openEnd`.
- Out of scope: reverse and ping-pong playback.
- Tests: `packages/react/test/loop.test.mjs`,
  `packages/react/test/sequence.test.mjs`. Example: `examples/loop-repeat`.
